About AnimeChart

Welcome to AnimeChart.

AnimeChart is an anime information and tracking website designed to help anime fans discover anime, follow upcoming episodes and check airing schedules.

What We Provide

  • Anime information
  • Upcoming episode information
  • Anime airing schedules
  • Weekly anime schedules
  • Anime search and discovery

Our Goal

Our goal is to make it easier for anime fans to keep track of their favorite shows and upcoming episodes in one simple place.

Data Sources

Anime information and related data may be provided through third-party APIs and services.
